About IAA Journal

The IAA Journal is the official monthly digital publication of Intercontinental Aviation Academy (FlyIAA) — a leading aviation training institution based in the UAE, offering EASA-compliant pilot programmes, Type Rating courses, and career development pathways. Each issue covers pilot training updates, student achievements, industry news, and insights from aviation professionals worldwide.
